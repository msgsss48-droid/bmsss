About Us

Bermiok Martam Sr. Sec. School is a government senior secondary school located at Bermiok Martam, Gyalshing District, Sikkim. With a rich legacy of serving the community, the school is committed to providing quality, inclusive and holistic education to children from diverse backgrounds.

Our school believes that education is not limited to academic achievement. We strive to nurture students into confident, responsible and compassionate individuals by providing opportunities in academics, sports, arts, technology, cultural activities and community engagement.

With dedicated teachers, supportive staff and a student-centred learning environment, we encourage every learner to discover their potential, develop critical thinking and build the skills required to face the challenges of the future.

The school continues to embrace modern technology and innovative teaching practices while preserving the values, traditions and cultural heritage of Sikkim. We work closely with parents and the community to create a safe, positive and inspiring environment for every student.

Our Vision

To nurture responsible, confident and lifelong learners who contribute positively to society and become capable leaders of tomorrow.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality education in an inclusive and supportive environment.
  • To develop academic excellence along with creativity, discipline and critical thinking.
  • To encourage participation in sports, cultural activities and community service.
  • To promote digital literacy, innovation and 21st-century skills.
  • To instil values of integrity, respect, responsibility and compassion.
  • To empower every student to realise their full potential.
Our Motto: “Arise to Inspire”
